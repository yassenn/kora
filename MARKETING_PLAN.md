# Kora: Marketing Strategy & Growth Projections (2026)

## 1. Executive Summary
Kora is a hyper-local soccer match-making and pitch management platform designed to bridge the gap between amateur players and venue owners. Our goal is to become the "Uber for Soccer," making it as easy to book a pitch and find a game as it is to order a ride.

## 2. Target Audience
*   **The Weekend Warrior (18-35):** Casual players looking for consistent games without the hassle of organizing.
*   **The Professional Organizer:** People who manage local leagues or groups and need better tools for attendance and payment.
*   **The Venue Owner:** Small-to-medium pitch owners looking to maximize occupancy during off-peak hours.
*   **The Student Athlete:** High school and college students looking for competitive pick-up games.

## 3. Brand Positioning
**"Play Anywhere. Anytime."**
Kora stands for community, health, and the love of the game. Our brand voice is energetic, inclusive, and community-focused.

## 4. Marketing Channels & Tactics

### Phase 1: The Grassroots Launch (Months 1-3)
*   **Hyper-Local SEO:** Optimize for keywords like "soccer pitches near me" or "pick-up soccer [City Name]."
*   **Pitch Partnerships:** Install physical QR code banners at partner pitches. "Download Kora to book this pitch instantly."
*   **Ambassador Program:** Recruit 5 influential local players per city to host "Kora Featured Matches" with free entry for the first 10 users.

### Phase 2: Community & Content (Months 4-9)
*   **Social Media (Instagram/TikTok):** Focus on "Goal of the Week" user-generated content (UGC).
*   **Referral Loop:** "Refer a friend, get $5 off your next pitch booking."
*   **Leagues & Tournaments:** Organize Kora-sponsored "One-Day Cups" to drive mass registration.

### Phase 3: Scaling & Retention (Months 10+)
*   **Push Notification Retargeting:** Use FCM to remind users of upcoming games or open spots in their favorite venues.
*   **Gamification:** Implement player ratings and badges (e.g., "Top Scorer," "Reliable Teammate").
*   **Corporate Partnerships:** Partner with local businesses for "Company Match Days."

### Key Growth Metrics (KPIs)
*   **CAC (Cost Per Acquisition):** Target < $2.50 via organic social and pitch QR codes.
*   **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Target $45/year based on booking fees and premium features.
*   **Retention Rate:** Target > 40% (users playing at least 1 match per month).

## 6. Competitive Advantage
Unlike generic booking apps, Kora focuses specifically on the **social dynamics** of soccer (invitations, player roles, and match types), creating a community rather than just a utility.
