# Kora: Financial Projections & Budget (Year 1)

## 1. Initial Setup Budget (Capital Expenditure)
Estimated total for launch: **$15,000**

| Item | Description | Estimated Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cloud Infrastructure** | AWS/DigitalOcean setup, SSL, Load Balancers | $1,500 |
| **Legal & Compliance** | Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, GDPR Audit | $3,500 |
| **Brand Identity** | Professional Logo, UI/UX refinement, Assets | $2,000 |
| **Marketing Materials** | Pitch Banners (QR Codes), Physical Signage | $4,000 |
| **Initial Working Capital** | Emergency fund, small tool subscriptions | $4,000 |

## 2. Monthly Operational Expenses (OpEx)
Estimated at full scale (Month 12): **$8,500 / month**

*   **Hosting & API Fees:** $800 (Server, FCM, SendGrid, Maps API)
*   **Marketing & CAC:** $5,000 (Social media ads, Influencer fees)
*   **Maintenance & Support:** $2,000 (Freelance bug fixes, Customer support)
*   **Misc (Insurance/Software):** $700

## 3. Revenue Streams
*   **Pitch Booking Fee:** 5% commission on every transaction processed through the app.
*   **Premium Organizer Tier:** $9.99/mo for advanced league management tools.
*   **In-App Advertising:** Featured pitch listings for venue owners ($50/week).

## 6. Financial Assumptions
*   **Conversion Rate:** 15% of active users make at least 2 bookings per month.
*   **Average Booking Value:** $60 per pitch hour.
*   **Taxes:** Calculated based on a standard 20% corporate tax rate (varies by region).
*   **Transaction Costs:** Net profit accounts for a 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing fee (Stripe/PayPal) which is deducted from gross revenue.
